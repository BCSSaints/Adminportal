Domino’s Fundraiser Notice

Domino’s Slice the Price Card Update & Refund Details

Description

We are grateful for the support our families have shown through the Domino’s Slice the Price card fundraiser over the years. It has truly been a blessing to both our school and our families. Due to a recent management change at our local Domino’s locations, the stores will no longer be able to accept the cards. This change was unexpected and outside of our control. If you purchased a card, you are eligible for a full refund. There is no need to return the card. Simply call Domino’s fundraising customer service at 866-252-6103, let them know you are with Byne Christian School and that your local stores are no longer honoring the cards due to a management change, and request a refund. The amount will be credited back to the original form of payment. We appreciate your understanding and continued support of our school’s fundraising efforts. Your partnership makes a lasting difference for our students and campus.

Don’t Miss It — Help Us Serve Our Community

Description

Our Thespian Troupe is partnering with Sleep in Heavenly Peace to help meet a real need in our community—and you can be part of it.
